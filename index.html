More than a crown.
A celebration of individuality, confidence and purpose.

Queen of the Baltic brings together women from different cultures to celebrate beauty, self-expression and personal ambition. It is a space to be seen, to share a story and to contribute to something meaningful.

Discover our purpose

In the name of charity

True beauty lives at the heart of our contest.

We created this project to unite women of the Baltic region and to show that true beauty is more than appearance — it’s character, individuality, talent and kindness. Here women step onto the stage, work with industry professionals, support charity and reach international platforms — valued for their age, culture and inner depth.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Write to contest@qotb.eu

01Who can apply?

Women from the Baltic region and beyond who want to express themselves on stage — the contest values character, individuality, talent and kindness, not appearance alone.

02How do the categories work?

Contestants compete in separate age categories, so every woman is measured among her peers. In 2026 the crowns went to Juta Bendi (Category 1), Jane Doe (Category 2) and Luiza Romanova (Category 3).

03When does the 2027 season start?

Dates and the venue for 2027 will be announced soon. Apply now and we will contact you as soon as the casting opens.

04What does participation include?

Preparation with industry professionals, photo sessions, stage rehearsals, a charity programme and the final show in Tallinn.

05Where does the charity support go?

Together with partner foundations we support children in difficult life situations. Every season dedicates part of its programme to this cause.

06How can my brand become a partner?

Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Queen of the Baltic International respects your privacy. Below is what personal data we collect through qotb.eu, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

01Who we are

Queen of the Baltic International, Tallinn, Estonia, is the controller of personal data processed through this website. Contact: contest@qotb.eu, +372 5592 1134.

02What we collect

Data you send us in the application and partnership forms — name, email, country, company and your message — and, only with your consent, anonymised statistics about how the site is used.

03Why we use it

To answer your request, run the casting and the contest, and communicate with partners (contract and legitimate interest), and to improve the website (consent).

04Cookies and storage

We store your cookie choice in your browser. Analytics cookies are set only after you click “Accept” and can be refused by choosing “Essential only”.

05Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the contest and the website (hosting, email, analytics), under data-processing agreements. We never sell personal data.

06How long we keep it

Application data is kept for the current and the following season, partnership correspondence for as long as the cooperation lasts, unless the law requires longer.

07Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee).

Questions about your data: contest@qotb.eu
