About

Kseniya Petrova
Founder of Queen of The Baltic
{{ s.icon }}

Ksenia Petrova is the founder of the international beauty and cultural project
Queen of the Baltic International, entrepreneur and initiator of cultural and social initiatives.

Having experienced the international pageant world firsthand while representing Estonia on the global stage, she created Queen of the Baltic as a modern platform that brings together women from different countries and generations.

The project was designed as a space where beauty meets intelligence, leadership and social impact. Charity and social responsibility are essential pillars of the competition’s philosophy.

Founder’s Manifesto

«For me, beauty is not only about appearance.
It is a woman’s energy, her character, her voice and her ability to change the world around her.

Queen of the Baltic was created as a space where women can reveal their strength, their story and their influence.»

— Ksenia Petrova
